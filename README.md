# Smart Finance

## Overview

Smart Finance is a personal financial management web application designed to help users manage and monitor their financial activities in an easy and organized way. The system allows users to record their income, track expenses, manage debts and credits, and monitor their savings progress.

The main goal of Smart Finance is to help users understand their financial situation and make better financial decisions.

---

## Features

### Income Management

Users can record and manage all sources of income, including salaries, business profits, and other earnings.

### Expense Tracking

The system helps users record daily, weekly, or monthly expenses to monitor spending habits and control unnecessary expenditures.

### Debt and Credit Management

Users can keep records of debts borrowed or lent, helping them track payments and balances effectively.

### Savings Progress Tracking

Smart Finance enables users to create savings goals and monitor their progress over time.

### Financial Analysis and Advice

The application analyzes financial records and provides useful insights, recommendations, and advice to help users improve their financial management and planning.

---

## Objectives

* Help users organize their financial information.
* Improve financial decision-making.
* Encourage saving habits.
* Reduce unnecessary spending.
* Provide a simple and user-friendly financial management platform.
